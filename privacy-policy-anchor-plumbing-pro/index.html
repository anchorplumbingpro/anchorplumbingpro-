1. Who we are

This website is operated by Anchor Plumbing Pro, 2500 Pleasanton Rd, San Antonio, TX 78221, USA, telephone (210) 944-4234. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Anchor Plumbing Pro.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Service request form: your name, phone number, email address (optional), service ZIP code, the type of service and property, and the description you write.
  • Phone calls, texts and emails: whatever you tell us, including your service address and details about your plumbing problem.

Information collected automatically

  • Our web host and security tools may log technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, pages requested and date and time.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google when a page loads.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data through this site, and ask that you do not send payment card numbers, government ID numbers or passwords through the form.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your request, schedule service and give you estimates.
  • To provide, manage and follow up on plumbing work.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website and to prevent spam and fraud.
  • To keep records and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the site and handle requests. Form submissions are processed by Formspree, which receives what you type into the form and delivers it to us. Our website host also processes technical data.
  • Professionals and partners only when needed to carry out your request, such as scheduling or completing your service.
  • Authorities or other parties where required by law, to enforce our rights, or to protect safety.
  • A successor if our business is sold or reorganized, subject to this policy.

5. Cookies and tracking

We do not currently set advertising or analytics cookies on this site. If that changes we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent. Your browser may set its own technical storage, and third-party services we load (such as Google Fonts) may process data under their own policies.

6. How long we keep information

We keep service request and job records for as long as needed to respond, provide service, keep business and tax records, and meet legal requirements, then delete or de-identify them.

7. Your Texas privacy rights

The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) gives Texas residents certain rights over their personal data, subject to the law's scope and exceptions. Depending on whether the Act applies to us, you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal data and give you access to it;
  • correct inaccuracies in your personal data;
  • delete personal data you provided or that we obtained about you;
  • give you a copy of your personal data in a portable format where feasible;
  • opt out of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising, or profiling for significant decisions. We do none of these.

To make a request, call us or use the contact details in section 13. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within 45 days and may extend that once by up to 45 days when reasonably necessary, and we will tell you why. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision; if you disagree with the outcome of an appeal, you may contact the Texas Attorney General at texasattorneygeneral.gov. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as required by Texas law, including Chapter 521 of the Texas Business and Commerce Code.

9. Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Calls, texts and email

If you submit the form and tick the consent box, or call us, we may contact you by phone, text or email about your request. Consent is not a condition of buying any service. Message and data rates may apply to texts. You can ask us to stop at any time by telling us or replying STOP to a text. We follow the Telephone Consumer Protection Act and the CAN-SPAM Act. We do not share phone numbers or text-messaging consent with third parties for their marketing.

11. Third-party links

Our pages may link to other websites, such as SAWS, mapping services and the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be posted on this page.
