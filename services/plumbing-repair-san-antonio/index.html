Common plumbing problems and possible repairs

Symptoms can overlap, so identifying the actual cause matters more than guessing at a replacement part. These are some of the repair categories homeowners commonly ask about.

Pipe leaks and damaged connections

Moisture under sinks, at supply lines or near exposed piping may indicate a worn fitting, corroded section or damaged connection.

Learn about leak detection →

Faucets, sinks and fixtures

Persistent dripping, loose connections and inconsistent flow may be linked to cartridges, seals, aerators or supply fittings.

Toilets that run, leak or clog

Flappers, fill valves, seals and drain obstructions are common areas to inspect when a toilet does not operate normally.

Low or inconsistent water pressure

Pressure issues may involve a fixture, shutoff valve, pressure regulator or supply pipe. The location of the problem helps guide diagnosis.

Drain and sewer issues

Repeated clogs, gurgling and multiple slow fixtures may require drain troubleshooting or a camera inspection.

Early warning signs

When to investigate a plumbing issue

Even small symptoms can help identify where a plumbing system needs attention.

  • Unexplained dampness, water stains or musty odors
  • Visible drips under sinks or near appliance supply lines
  • Water pressure that changes without a clear reason
  • Toilets that refill repeatedly or run between flushes
  • Discolored water or signs of corrosion on exposed piping
  • Multiple fixtures draining slowly at the same time
Understanding the process

How a plumbing repair is typically assessed

Exact diagnostic steps depend on the property and the symptoms. A methodical inspection can help avoid unnecessary work.

01

Describe and locate

Identify affected fixtures, the timing of the problem and whether water is actively leaking.

02

Inspect and test

Depending on the issue, a professional may inspect valves, connections, pressure, drainage or accessible pipework.

03

Review the repair plan

Discuss the suspected cause, recommended work, access requirements and follow-up checks before proceeding.

A practical decision

Targeted repair or larger pipe replacement?

A localized failure may be suitable for a focused repair. Repeated leaks, widespread corrosion or deteriorating piping may justify discussing a broader replacement plan.

  • Consider how often the same problem has returned.
  • Ask about the age and material of existing pipes.
  • Understand which areas need access for the work.
  • Confirm how the system will be tested afterward.

Before calling

What to tell a plumbing professional

Useful details make it easier to explain the issue and discuss an appropriate next step.

  • Which room or fixture is affected?
  • When did the symptom begin?
  • Is the problem constant or intermittent?
  • Are nearby fixtures also affected?
  • Is water actively escaping or shut off?

Avoid opening walls, disassembling gas connections or handling electrical equipment near water without appropriate qualifications.

Important safety guidance

If a supply line bursts, shut off the main water valve if safe. Keep away from standing water near outlets or electrical equipment. For a suspected gas leak, leave immediately and contact your utility or emergency services from outside.

Plumbing repair FAQs

Questions about plumbing repairs in San Antonio

What are common signs that a pipe needs repair?

Watch for persistent dripping, visible corrosion, damp drywall, reduced pressure, unexplained moisture and repeated leaks. An inspection helps identify whether the pipe or a nearby connection is responsible.

Why does my water pressure suddenly drop?

Possible causes include a partially closed valve, fixture blockage, supply-line leak or a broader water service issue. Check whether one fixture or the entire property is affected.

Can a leaking faucet or toilet waste water?

Yes. Continuous drips and running toilets can use water even when fixtures are not in use. Worn seals, valves and internal components are common causes.

How do I shut off the water during a plumbing emergency?

Locate the main water shutoff valve and close it if accessible and safe. If water is near electrical equipment, avoid the area and seek qualified help.

Should an older plumbing system be repaired or repiped?

Consider pipe material, number of past leaks, extent of damage, access and remaining service life. A qualified plumber can explain targeted repair versus repiping.
