Know the warning signs

Small plumbing symptoms can signal bigger problems

Look beyond the obvious drip. Changes in pressure, drainage and water temperature may help narrow down what needs attention.

  • Water stains or damp cabinets near sinks and supply lines
  • Recurring clogs or gurgling drains
  • Sudden changes in water pressure
  • Water heater producing inconsistent hot water
  • Unexplained moisture around floors or walls
  • Scale buildup around faucets and fixtures

Helpful answers

Frequently asked plumbing questions

What plumbing services are covered in San Antonio?

Topics include plumbing repairs, drains, sewer inspections, water heaters, leak detection, repiping, water softening and filtration. Call to confirm the service and address are supported.

How can I tell whether a plumbing problem is urgent?

Active flooding, a burst pipe, sewage backing up into living spaces or suspected gas leakage calls for prompt action. Shut off water only if safe. For suspected gas leaks, leave and contact your utility or emergency services.

What information should I have ready when I call?

Describe the symptoms, where the problem occurs, when it started, whether multiple fixtures are affected and whether the water has been shut off.

Do you provide service throughout San Antonio?

Service coverage depends on the address and requested work. Consult the service-area guide and confirm coverage when calling.

Should I repair or replace aging pipes?

The decision depends on pipe material, age, access, repeated failures and inspection findings. A qualified professional can explain the available options.
